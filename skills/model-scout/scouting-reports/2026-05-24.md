# Model Scout Report — 2026-05-24

**Goal:** Find a model that is powerful and fast for general chat and coding,
runs well on 2× DGX Spark (GB10, TP=2), and provides at least 32 GiB headroom
per node vs. the current ~11 GiB.

**Hardware budget:** 0.90 × 121 GiB = 108.9 GiB/node
**Target max weights/node:** 108.9 − 32 = 76.9 GiB
**Currently running:** Step-3.5-Flash-FP8 (~97.5 GiB/node, ~11 GiB headroom)

---

## Candidates

### ⭐ Llama 4 Scout 17B-16E FP8 — Most Promising

- **HuggingFace:** https://huggingface.co/RedHatAI/Llama-4-Scout-17B-16E-Instruct-FP8-dynamic
- **Total params:** 109B
- **Active params/token:** 17B
- **FP8 footprint (estimated):** ~109 GiB total → ~54.5 GiB/node at TP=2
- **Headroom/node:** ~54 GiB ✅ (well above 32 GiB target)
- **Context:** 524K tokens
- **vLLM:** compatible from 0.8.3+; vLLM 0.19 is fine
- **Architecture:** MoE, 16 experts
- **Released:** April 2025 (Meta)

**Why interesting:** 17B active params per token is substantially more compute
than the sparse MoE options below. Strong benchmark scores for chat and coding.
524K context is a real upgrade over current 32K cap.

**Flags before downloading:**
- Red Hat tested on 4–8 GPUs; need to confirm no minimum TP requirement
  — TP=2 at ~54.5 GiB/node should be within budget but not verified
- SM 12.1 (GB10) compatibility unknown — check vLLM issue tracker and 26.04
  release notes before committing
- Do NOT pass `--quantization fp8` (checkpoint already quantized — learned lesson)

---

### Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking FP8

- **HuggingFace:** https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B-Thinking-FP8
- **Total params:** 80B
- **Active params/token:** ~3B (512 experts, top-10 routing, high-sparsity MoE)
- **FP8 footprint (estimated):** ~80 GiB total → ~40 GiB/node at TP=2
- **Headroom/node:** ~69 GiB ✅ (enormous)
- **Context:** 262K native (extensible to 1M with YaRN)
- **Architecture:** Hybrid DeltaNet+MoE, 48 layers
- **Released:** ~April 2026 (Qwen team)

**Why interesting:** Huge headroom, thinking model, very long native context.

**Concerns:** Only 3B active params per token is very light — likely less capable
than Scout for complex coding tasks. Benchmarks place it at #112 of 525 models
(Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index score: 27 vs. average 15 for comparable
models). Good for analytical/math tasks; unclear for hard coding.

---

### Qwen3.6-27B FP8 (dense)

- **HuggingFace:** https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B-FP8
- **Total params:** 27B (dense)
- **FP8 footprint (estimated):** ~27 GiB total → ~13.5 GiB/node at TP=2
- **Headroom/node:** ~95 GiB ✅ (leaves almost nothing used)
- **Context:** TBD — needs checking
- **Released:** 2026 (Qwen team)

**Why interesting:** Trivial memory footprint, massive headroom for KV cache and
other workloads. Dense architecture means no MoE sparsity tradeoffs.

**Concerns:** Dense 27B is a meaningful capability step down from the current
196B MoE. Best suited if raw throughput and headroom matter more than model
quality. Worth comparing benchmarks before committing.
